For My Soul Sister 🤍

Some people enter your life quietly…
and somehow become a part of your forever.

I never really planned on finding a soul sister when I came to university.

I thought I was just going to meet classmates, make some friends, survive assignments, complain about university, make memories, and eventually move on.

And then there was you.

Somewhere between ordinary university days, random conversations, stupid jokes, shared moments, and all the little things that probably didn’t seem important at the time…

I got attached.

And apparently, I got attached way too much because now there is no going back. 😭😂

What started as “we’re just classmates” somehow became “you’re stuck with me for life.”

And honestly? I wouldn’t have it any other way.

You were unexpected.

That’s probably what makes this friendship so special to me.

I didn’t know when it happened. There wasn’t one particular moment where I thought, “Yep, this person is going to become my soul sister.”

It just happened.

You slowly became someone I wanted to talk to, someone I wanted around, someone I could be completely myself with.

And one day I realized… you’re not just my university friend anymore.

You’re family.

Not because we have known each other forever, but because somehow, in the time we’ve known each other, you’ve become someone my heart feels incredibly comfortable with.

Our friendship doesn’t need a perfect story.

We don’t need some dramatic story about how we met. We don’t need thousands of pictures or extraordinary adventures to prove that we’re close.

Some of my favourite memories are probably the simplest ones.

The random conversations.
The unnecessary laughter.
The stupid jokes that somehow become funny only to us.

The moments where nothing special was happening, but having you there made the moment special anyway.

That’s the kind of friendship I treasure.

The kind that feels natural.
The kind where you don’t have to try too hard.
The kind where you can just be yourself.

And somehow, that’s enough.

So, on your birthday…

I just want you to know that I’m genuinely grateful for you.

Grateful that university introduced me to you.
Grateful for every conversation, every laugh, every random moment, every memory we’ve made so far.

And most importantly, grateful that an ordinary university friendship somehow turned into something I hope lasts far beyond university.

I hope life takes us to beautiful places. I hope we both grow, succeed, change, and become better versions of ourselves.

But I also hope that somewhere along the way, we never lose this.

The comfort.
The craziness.
The random conversations.
The effortless friendship.
The ability to talk about absolutely nothing and somehow make it feel important.

And years from now, when university is just a chapter we look back on, I hope we can still say:

“Remember when we first met?”

And then laugh at how neither of us knew that we were going to become this close.

A little birthday prayer for you 🤍

May Allah fill your life with happiness that stays.
May He give you success without taking away your peace.
May He protect your heart from things that aren’t meant for you.
May He bring you people who genuinely love, respect and value you.
May He make your dreams easier to reach and your difficult days easier to survive.

And may every year of your life bring you closer to the person you’re meant to become.

Ameen. 🤍

And one last thing…

I don’t know what the future looks like. I don’t know where life will take either of us.

But I do know one thing: you accidentally became one of the most precious people in my life.

And unfortunately for you… there is no unsubscribe button. 😭😂

No cancellation.
No trial period.
No return policy.

The lifetime contract has officially been signed. 🤝🏻❤️

So here’s to you.
To your birthday.
To our university days.
To all the memories we’ve already made.
And to all the ones we haven’t made yet.

Happy Birthday, my soul sister. 🤍

I’m really, really glad I found you.

And I’m even more glad that somehow… you found me too.

— With love,
Your permanently attached soul sister 🫶🏻
